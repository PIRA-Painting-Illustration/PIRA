A painting app for
pen tablets.

PIRA is a free drawing and painting app for Android pen tablets. Pencils and brushes respond to pressure and tilt as soon as you install it, and it works offline with no account. Later, artists will be able to share or sell their own brush packs.

Explore the roadmapNo APK or public beta is available yet.

What comes first

We are working on the pencil stroke, the canvas and layered files first. The long list of extra features other editors have can wait.

01 · Works offline

You don't need to log in, activate a licence or connect to the internet to draw, save or export.

02 · Made for the pen

Brushes use pen pressure and tilt from the start, and the touch gestures were tested on real tablets.

03 · Open source

Anyone can read the code. The file and brush formats are documented, and you can import your own brushes.

Brush packs from other artists

You'll be able to download free brush packs, or buy packs from the artists who made them. The drawing tools stay free, and packs you buy keep working offline.

Free packs

Publish a brush pack under an open licence and get credited whenever someone uses it.

Paid packs

Artists can choose to sell their packs. The terms and moderation will be settled before any sales open.

Your files stay yours

Your paintings are never uploaded automatically. The brush-pack features are kept separate from the painting engine.

Roadmap

The planned milestones. They may change after testing and feedback.

0 · Research

Licences, tablet testing, performance, and talking to artists.

1 · Canvas

Fast rendering, pencils and inks with pressure and tilt, and saving files.

2 · Free beta

Brush settings, layers, crash recovery and a first set of free packs.

3 · Marketplace

Paid packs, once the rules and the seller setup have been reviewed.

Status

PIRA is an independent project, unrelated to ArtCraft or Clip Studio Paint. It is built on PhotoCraft, which is open source under the MIT or Apache 2.0 licence.

“PIRA” is a working name until the trademark has been checked. The code is on GitHub in a private repository for now. Nothing is for sale yet, and there are no seller accounts or public downloads.
Early prototype · October 2026. The app, the marketplace and the public repository haven't launched yet.
