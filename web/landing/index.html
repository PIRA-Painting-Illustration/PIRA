Open-source concept • Planning phase

Paint freely.
Keep your canvas.

A professional drawing and painting app built for artists, not subscriptions. Offline by design, expressive with a stylus, and supported by a community that can share or sell its own brush packs.

Explore the roadmapNo APK or public beta is available yet.

Built around the act of drawing.

We will prioritize a great pencil stroke, responsive canvas and dependable layered documents before chasing feature-count parity with established editors.

01 · Offline ownership

No mandatory login, license activation or cloud connection to draw, save and export.

02 · Stylus-first

Pressure and tilt controls built into compatible brushes by default, with gestures designed for real tablets.

03 · Open foundations

Auditable source, documented file and brush formats, local imports and transparent development decisions.

Community is optional. Creativity isn't.

Download free community brushes or support creators by purchasing their packs. Every core drawing feature remains free; even purchased packs continue to work offline once installed.

Free sharing

Publish brush packs under an explicit open-content license and receive clear attribution.

Artist-owned sales

Opt-in commercial listings with transparent terms, creator ownership and a planned moderation process.

Your files stay local

No automatic uploads of your canvases. Community services remain isolated from the paint engine.

Build it in the right order.

Proposed milestones, subject to technical testing and community feedback.

0 · Research

Licenses, hardware trace, performance and artist interviews.

1 · Canvas

Rust renderer, pencil/ink, pressure, tilt and local files.

2 · Free beta

Brush Studio, layers, recovery and curated free packs.

3 · Marketplace

Optional paid resources only after policy and seller-readiness reviews.

Independent by design.

This is an early-stage product proposal, not an official ArtCraft or Clip Studio Paint release. The project may reuse permissively licensed PhotoCraft technology subject to its license terms and independent branding.

Project name “PIRA” is provisional pending trademark clearance. The official GitHub organization/repository URL will be added after creation. No payments, seller accounts, or downloads are operating at this stage.
