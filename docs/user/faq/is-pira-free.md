---
title: Is PIRA free?
description: PIRA is free and open source under the MIT or Apache 2.0 license, and works offline with no account.
order: 10
---

Yes. PIRA is free, with no subscription and no account. The code is licensed under MIT or Apache 2.0, your choice.

PIRA works offline. Drawing, saving and exporting never need the internet, and its smart selection tools run on your device, with no cloud.

The PIRA logo and name are not covered by the code licence.

See [Install PIRA](../getting-started/install.md) to download it.
