# PIRA wiki

PIRA is a free painting and illustration app for pen tablets. It works offline, with no account
or subscription. Pencils, inks and paints respond to pen pressure and tilt from the moment you
install it. It runs on Android tablets first, and also on Windows and Linux.

PIRA is an early release. Keep copies of work that matters.
