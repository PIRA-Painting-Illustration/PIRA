# Pens, pencils and brushes

## The three families

PIRA's starter brushes are split into three tools on the touch bar:

| Tool | What it's for | How the pen drives it |
|---|---|---|
| **Pen** | Solid inks and line art | Pressure sets the width; lines are steadied |
| **Pencil** | Sketching with paper grain | Pressure darkens; tilt shades with the side of the lead |
| **Brush** | Watercolour, oil, blending, mixing | Paints through the Mixer Brush, picking up colour already on the canvas |

Each tool remembers the last brush you picked in it. Its brush picker shows that family's brushes
and any brushes you've added yourself.

## Pressure and tilt

- **Pressure for Size** and **Pressure for Opacity** (options bar) show whether pen pressure
  changes the stroke's width and its opacity. Turn one off to stop pressure affecting that.
- Tilt shades pencils and broadens their marks, like using the side of the lead.
- If a stroke ignores pressure, check these toggles first, then the pen itself (see
  [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting-and-FAQ.md)).

## Mixing brushes

The Brush tool's paints use the **Mixer Brush**. Its options bar has:

- **Wet:** how much colour the brush picks up from the canvas.
- **Load:** how much paint the brush holds before it runs dry.
- **Mix:** how much of the canvas colour mixes into the brush's colour.
- **Flow:** how quickly paint goes down.
- **Sample All Layers:** mix with what's on every layer, not just the current one.

## Smart Shape

Hold the pen still at the end of a Brush, Pencil or Eraser stroke and it snaps to a straight
line, an ellipse or a rectangle. Keep dragging before you lift to adjust it. Turn it on or off with
**Smart Shape (hold to snap)** in the options bar.

## Your own brushes

- **Import brushes:** use **Import Brushes…** in the brush picker's gear menu, or the folder
  button in the Brushes panel. It takes Photoshop `.abr` files, or an image to use as a brush tip.
  Imported brushes appear among your own.
- **Brush settings:** brush tips, shape dynamics, scattering, texture, dual brush, colour jitter
  and transfer, as in Photoshop.

## Drawing straight lines and shapes

- Hold Shift (on the keyboard or the Shortcut Pad) to draw a straight line from the last point.
- Use [rulers](Rulers-and-Guides.md) to guide strokes along perspective lines, curves, circles
  and more.
