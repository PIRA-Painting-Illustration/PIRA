# Starting a canvas

**File › New…** opens the New dialog. It remembers the settings you used last time.

## What you can set

- **Presets:** screen sizes, print sizes, and **Comic** pages (see below).
- **Width, height and resolution**, in pixels or print units.
- **Colour mode and bit depth:** RGB, greyscale, CMYK or Lab; 8, 16 or 32 bits.
- **Background contents:** white, black, the background colour, transparent, or one of 20 paper
  colours shown as chips.
- **Record timelapse:** starts a [timelapse](Timelapse.md) from the first stroke.

## What you get

A new canvas opens with two layers:

- **Background** at the bottom, filled with the paper colour you chose.
- **Layer 1** above it: empty, selected and ready to draw on.

So your first strokes never land on the paper itself, and you can change the paper colour later
without touching the drawing. With a transparent background there's no Background layer, only an
empty Layer 1.

## Comic pages

The Comic presets make a page ready for print: manga B5, US comic, A4, square and webtoon. The
canvas is the trim size plus the bleed, and guides mark the trim line and the safe area inside it.
See [Comic pages](Comic-Pages.md) for panels, balloons and tones.

## Other ways to start

- **File › Open** an image or a PSD, or drop a file onto the window (desktop).
- **File › New from Clipboard** makes a canvas the size of the copied image.
- **File › Open Recent** lists files you've worked on.
