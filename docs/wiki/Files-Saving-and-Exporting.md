# Files, saving and exporting

## PIRA's own format: .pcraft

Save your work as `.pcraft` (**File › Save**). It keeps everything:

- every layer, mask, style and setting,
- [3D layers](3D-Figures.md) as live, re-posable scenes,
- [special rulers](Rulers-and-Guides.md), guides and comic page settings,
- the line-art layer settings (reference, draft, line colour, border),
- the [timelapse](Timelapse.md) recording.

## Photoshop files

- PIRA opens and saves layered **PSD** and **PSB** files, with layers, masks, adjustment layers,
  text and most layer styles.
- PIRA-only layers (3D, for example) are saved into a PSD as pixels, and PIRA warns you when it
  does.
- Keep a `.pcraft` copy of anything you want to keep editing in PIRA.

## Exporting images

- **File › Export › Export As…** saves PNG, JPEG, WebP, TIFF, GIF and more, with size and quality
  options.
- **File › Export › Quick Export as PNG** saves a PNG in one step.
- [Draft layers](Layers.md) are left out of exports.

## Saving on Android

Android saves through its own file picker:

- **Save As** and the exports open the system's save picker, where you choose a folder (on the
  tablet, in Drive and so on) and a name.
- PIRA keeps the name you typed and its permission to write to that file, so **Save** afterwards
  writes straight back to it.
- Commands that need a whole folder at once aren't available on Android, except
  [Render Video](Animation-and-Render-Video.md), which saves a single MP4 or GIF instead.

## Recovery

PIRA autosaves open documents in the background. If PIRA closes unexpectedly, it offers to
recover them the next time it starts.

## Video

- [Timelapse](Timelapse.md) exports an MP4 of your painting process.
- [Render Video](Animation-and-Render-Video.md) exports a timeline animation.
