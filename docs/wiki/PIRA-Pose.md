# PIRA Pose

PIRA Pose is PIRA's 3D mannequin as a free app of its own, for Android tablets and Windows. It's
for when you only need a reference figure. It shares PIRA's posing code, so both apps pose, light
and fall the same way.

## What it has

- Male and female mannequins, up to 64 figures in a scene.
- The same pose library: built-in poses, over a thousand motion-capture poses and hand poses.
- Match a pose to a photo, for the body or hands.
- **Gravity** with pins, push and muscle tone.
- Two lights, with floor shadows.
- A background colour (neutral grey by default) or a photo behind the figures.
- Dockable panels you can move, tab together, stack, dock left or right, or float.

## Exporting

- Export PNGs with a transparent background, or over your photo.
- Save scenes and come back to them later.

## PIRA or PIRA Pose?

Use PIRA Pose to set up a reference and export it as an image. Use PIRA's
[3D layers](3D-Figures.md) to pose the figure right inside the painting and draw over it.
